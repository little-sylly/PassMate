# About the study content

The files in `data/` hold the PassMate study content: vocabulary terms, practice
questions, question-wording drills and values scenarios, with Chinese
explanations in Simplified and Traditional script.

- The questions, drills, scenarios and Chinese text are original work,
  (c) 2026 Little Sylly, all rights reserved. The MIT licence in `LICENSE`
  covers the code only.
- Facts are checked against the Australian Government's testable booklet
  *Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond*, which is published under
  CC BY 4.0. The content is written in our own words.
- PassMate is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Australian Government.
